Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accommodate food allergies, dietary preferences, or sugar-free requests?

Items are prepared in a home kitchen that handles common allergens such as wheat/gluten, nuts, eggs, and dairy. Sugar-free and Gluten-friendly options are available for select items upon request. Please specify any sugar-free requests, allergy concerns, dietary preferences, or ingredient modifications in the order notes during checkout.

How do Porch Pickup and Market Pickup work?

Porch Pickup: Available on scheduled bake days (Sundays and Wednesdays) starting after 5:00 PM. You'll receive a confirmation with pickup details once your order is fresh and ready.
Market / Pop-Up Pickup: Simply select "Market Pickup" at checkout and let us know which event or date you'll be attending. Preordering guarantees your favorites won't sell out before you get there!

How should I store and reheat my cinnamon rolls and breads?

Cinnamon Rolls: Best enjoyed fresh! To reheat, pop a single roll in the microwave for 15–20 seconds to bring back that warm, gooey texture. They can also be frozen for up to 1 month.
Artisan Breads & Boules: Keep wrapped at room temperature for up to 3–4 days. To restore a crusty exterior, warm in a 350°F oven for 5–8 minutes.

When and how do I pay for my preorder?

We accept contactless electronic payments (Venmo, Cash App, or PayPal) prior to fulfillment, as well as cash on pickup/delivery. Payment details are confirmed after your order is received.

How do custom crochet/sewn orders and sizing work?

You can select your base item and leave your preferred colors, sizing (child, teen, adult), or character requests in the notes field at checkout. Custom craft turnaround times may vary depending on item complexity and current order volume.

What if I need to change or cancel my order?

Please reach out as soon as possible! Changes or cancellations must be made prior to the order cutoff window so ingredients and dough prep can be adjusted accordingly.
