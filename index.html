❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Helpful details about order pickups, ingredients, and crochet care.

How does local pickup and scheduling work?
Breads and treats are baked fresh on Sundays and Wednesdays. Once your order request is confirmed, you will receive an email or text with the pickup window and address. Please place orders 24–48 hours ahead so dough fermentation and prep can be scheduled properly.
What are your allergen and ingredient practices?
All baked goods are produced in a home kitchen. While we maintain strict cleanliness and can accommodate dairy-free requests on select loaves and items, our kitchen also processes wheat, eggs, dairy, and nuts. If you have severe allergies, please list them clearly in your order notes.
How do I care for and wash my crochet items?
  • Amigurumi Plushies: Spot clean with a damp cloth and mild soap. Air dry thoroughly.
  • Beanies & Scarves: Hand wash in cool water with gentle detergent, gently squeeze out excess water (do not wring), and lay flat to dry.
  • Cotton Dishcloths: Machine washable in warm water with like colors; tumble dry low or air dry.
How long do custom crochet orders take?
Most made-to-order plushies and accessories take 1 to 2 weeks depending on size, complexity, and current order volume. If you need an item by a specific date or event, mention it in your submission form and we'll let you know if rush turnaround is possible.
What payment methods do you accept?
We accept payment via Venmo, Cash App, PayPal, or exact cash upon pickup. Payment details and confirmation are shared once your order details and date are locked in.
