Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance do I need to place my order?

Because everything is made fresh from scratch, our order deadlines are Fridays by 5:00 PM for Sunday bakes and Mondays by 5:00 PM for Wednesday bakes. For custom crochet and knitted items, please allow 1–2 weeks for completion.

Can I pick up my order at the upcoming farmer's market?

Yes! Simply select "Market Pickup" on the preorder form and let us know which market date you'll be attending. We'll have your fresh box boxed and ready at our booth.

How do custom crochet/sewn orders work?

Select "Custom Crochet / Knitted Item" in the form below and describe what you're looking for (character beanie, blanket size, color preferences). We will reach out to confirm sizing, details, and price before getting started!

How should I store and reheat my cinnamon rolls and breads?

Cinnamon rolls are best enjoyed warm! Microwave a roll for 15–20 seconds to restore the gooey center. Boules and bread loaves keep fresh at room temperature wrapped tightly for 3–4 days, or can be sliced and frozen for up to 3 months.
